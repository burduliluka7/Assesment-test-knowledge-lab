# Statistical interpretation audit

- The scientific configuration and sensitivity grid are fixed by the user directive, not selected using downstream target recovery. The sensitivity study is one-factor-at-a-time at the final snapshot, conditional on the primary previous history.
- Independent coherence uses a different encoder family from construction. Null partitions preserve exact cluster size and per-cluster node-type counts. Observation and null include singletons identically. Absolute effects accompany standardized effects and discrete empirical p-values; a minimum p-value of 1/101 is resolution-limited, not zero.
- The 100 randomized null replicates measure variation conditional on one observed partition/type composition. Their Student-t mean interval is an interval for that conditional null mean, not for population scientific coherence. Null SD is separately exported. Multiple partition-level tests are descriptive and uncorrected.
- Perturbation CIs use five deletion seeds, with each current graph deleting a rounded 10% of edges. They describe deletion-seed variability on this corpus with fixed previous history. A t interval may extend outside the ARI range; intervals are not clipped or represented as guarantees. Five seeds provide limited precision.
- Real temporal averages pool three transitions and levels descriptively. Levels share nodes and transitions share snapshots, so these are not nine independent replications. No significance test treats them that way.
- Label NLI fractions, lexical support and comparison-work summaries include counts/means/SDs and generic t summaries in machine-readable output. Labels and fixed questions are dependent and not randomly sampled populations. These intervals are descriptive dispersion summaries, not calibrated binomial or population-inference guarantees.
- A high NLI overclaim proxy can reflect generation-only names absent from held-out text, truncation, model domain mismatch or an actual unsupported generalization. No causal decomposition of those effects or human truth labels is available.
- Direct node identity, composite document-component recovery, source overlap and strict mapped-claim evidence are distinct evaluation units. Partial targets do not earn complete-target credit. Unsupported or unevaluable entries stay explicit; they are not silently removed from raw denominators.
- Non-gold retrieved items are unjudged, not false positives. First-hit scan work excludes misses from its median and depends on ID order; total comparison work versus recall is the useful traversal comparison.
- No external held-out question set, scientific expert adjudication, latency-speedup claim or production-scaling claim is made. Determinism/reproduction establishes repeatability, not scientific validity.
