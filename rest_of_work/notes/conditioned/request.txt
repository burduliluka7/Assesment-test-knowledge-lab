# 3. CURRENT PROBLEM TO FIX

The current CR1 does:

```text
question
    ↓
old whole-question evidence selection
    ↓
small capped candidate evidence bundle
    ↓
question decomposition
    ↓
score each requirement against that already-selected bundle
```

This means evidence relevant to one requirement may have been discarded before requirement scoring even begins.

Example conceptual failure:

```text
Candidate = MACE

full graph evidence contains:

claim A:
accuracy close to DFT

claim B:
computational efficiency

claim C:
very-large-system scalability
```

but whole-question evidence selection keeps only:

```text
claim A
```

Then CR1 asks:

```text
accuracy?
efficiency?
scale?
```

against the same claim A.

That prevents requirement-aware scoring from realizing its full potential.

The new architecture should be:

```text
question
    ↓
generic question decomposition
    ↓
H2 top100 candidates
    ↓
for each candidate:
    for each task/requirement:
        search candidate's full eligible evidence pool
        specifically for that component
    ↓
attribute evidence
    ↓
aggregate per-requirement support
    ↓
rank candidates
```
